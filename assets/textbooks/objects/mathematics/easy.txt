Question 1:

D. 111

Question 2:
Express 0.004058 correct to 3 significant figures.
A. 0.004
B. 0.00406
C. 0.0041
D. 406

Answer: B
Explanation:
The first non-zero digit is 4. Counting three significant figures from there, we have 4, 0, 5. The next digit is 8 (which is 5 or greater), so we round up the last significant digit (5) to 6.
So, 0.004058 ≈ 0.00406.

Question 11:
In a class of 40 students, 25 speak Hausa, 16 speak Igbo, and 6 speak neither. How many students speak both languages?
A. 7
B. 8
C. 6
D. 9

Answer: A
Explanation:
Total students = 40.
Let H be Hausa speakers and I be Igbo speakers.
n(H∪I) = Total students - students who speak neither = 40 - 6 = 34.
n(H∪I) = n(H) + n(I) - n(H∩I)
34 = 25 + 16 - n(H∩I)
34 = 41 - n(H∩I)
n(H∩I) = 41 - 34 = 7.

Question 54:
If y varies directly as the square of x, and y = 32 when x = 4, find the value of y when x = 5.
A. 40
B. 50
C. 64
D. 100

Answer: B
Explanation:
y ∝ x² => y = kx² where k is the constant of proportionality.
Given y = 32 when x = 4:
32 = k(4)²
32 = 16k
k = 32 / 16 = 2
The formula is y = 2x².
When x = 5:
y = 2(5)² = 2(25) = 50.

Question 113:
A ladder 13m long leans against a vertical wall. The foot of the ladder is 5m away from the wall. How high up the wall does the ladder reach?
A. 8m
B. 10m
C. 12m
D. 18m

Answer: C
Explanation:
The ladder, the wall, and the ground form a right-angled triangle.
The ladder is the hypotenuse (13m).
The distance from the wall is one leg (5m).
The height up the wall (h) is the other leg.
By Pythagoras' theorem: h² + 5² = 13²
h² + 25 = 169
h² = 169 - 25 = 144
h = √144 = 12m.

Question 115:
A regular polygon has an interior angle of 144°. How many sides has the polygon?
A. 8
B. 9
C. 10
D. 12

Answer: C
Explanation:
Method 1: Using the exterior angle.
Exterior angle = 180° - Interior angle = 180° - 144° = 36°.
Number of sides (n) = 360° / Exterior angle
n = 360 / 36 = 10 sides.

Method 2: Using the interior angle formula.
Interior angle = [(n-2) × 180] / n
144 = [(n-2) × 180] / n
144n = 180n - 360
360 = 180n - 144n
360 = 36n
n = 360 / 36 = 10 sides.

Question 181:
In a right-angled triangle XYZ, angle Y = 90°, |XY| = 8cm and |XZ| = 10cm. Find tan(X).
A. 3/4
B. 4/3
C. 4/5
D. 3/5

Answer: A
Explanation:
First, find the length of the opposite side |YZ| using Pythagoras' theorem.
|XY|² + |YZ|² = |XZ|²
8² + |YZ|² = 10²
64 + |YZ|² = 100
|YZ|² = 100 - 64 = 36
|YZ| = 6cm.
Now, tan(X) = Opposite / Adjacent = |YZ| / |XY| = 6 / 8 = 3/4.

Question 182:
A town B is on a bearing of 060° from town A. A second town C is on a bearing of 150° from town A. If B and C are equidistant from A, find the bearing of C from B.
A. 195°
B. 150°
C. 210°
D. 240°

Answer: A
Explanation:
Draw a diagram. The angle between the path to B and the path to C at A is 150° - 60° = 90°.
Since AB = AC, triangle ABC is a right-angled isosceles triangle.
Angles ABC and ACB are (180 - 90)/2 = 45°.
To find the bearing of C from B, draw a North line at B parallel to the North line at A.
The angle of the line BA from B is 60° + 180° = 240°.
The bearing of C from B is the angle from North at B to the line BC.
Angle NBC = Angle NBA - Angle ABC = 240° - 45° = 195°.
Bearing of C from B is 195°.
